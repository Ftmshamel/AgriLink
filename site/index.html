Quick guide

Getting started, step by step

Pick your role to see what to do first.

  1. Create an accountTap Create account, choose Consumer, and pin your delivery address on the map.
  2. Cash in to your wallet (optional)Go to Profile → Wallet → Cash in and pay through GCash. You can also pay with GCash directly at checkout.
  3. Shop and add to cartBrowse by category or search. Set how many kilos you need; each crop has a minimum order.
  4. Choose when to deliverPick Same day or Schedule a date. The cart tells you if other buyers in your area share the trip, which lowers your delivery fee.
  5. Pay and trackPay with your wallet or GCash. Under Orders, follow the rider live on the map.
  6. Give your code, then confirmWhen the rider arrives, read out the 4-digit delivery code shown in your order. Check the items, then tap I received my order, or report a problem if something is wrong.

Good to know

  • Orders before 6 PM arrive the same day. Orders from 6 PM arrive the next day.
  • Scheduled orders can be cancelled up to 5 days before the delivery date.
  • Refunds always go back to your AgriLink wallet.
  • Rate the farm and the rider after delivery.

Safe delivery

How we know the order really arrived

A rider can't mark an order delivered just by tapping a button. Four checks back every drop-off.

GPS geofence

The rider's phone must be within 150 m of the buyer's pin. Fake GPS apps are blocked.

Delivery code

Only the buyer sees a 4-digit code. The rider has to enter it to finish the stop.

Photo proof

The rider takes a photo at pickup and at drop-off, saved with the time and place.

Buyer confirms

The buyer taps "I received my order" or reports a problem, which an admin then reviews.

Delivery and payment rules

The rules at a glance

Open 6 AM, cutoff 6 PMOrder before 6 PM for same-day delivery. Orders from 6 PM arrive the next day. Orders placed from midnight to 6 AM are still same-day, and riders start at 6 AM.
Scheduled deliveryChoose a later date. You can cancel up to 5 days before it. Same-day orders are final.
Shared trips cost lessWhen other buyers in your area order for the same day, the delivery fee is split. If someone joins after you paid, the difference goes back to your wallet.
AgriLink walletCash in through GCash and pay in one tap. Wallet money is for orders only and can't be withdrawn.
Or pay with GCash directlyYou can skip the wallet and pay each order through GCash.
Refunds go to your walletCancelled orders and approved reports are refunded to your wallet, whichever way you paid.

Install

Install on your phone

  1. Scan the QR code above, or tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open AgriLink.apk from your downloads.
  3. If Android says installs are blocked, tap Settings and allow this source.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Log in, or tap Create account and choose your role.
It says "App not installed"

An older AgriLink is already on your phone. Uninstall it first, then install the new one.

I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and follow the steps to set a new one.

Why does the app ask for my location?

To pin your farm or delivery address, to show nearby crops, and, for riders, to prove they reached each stop.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. AgriLink is available for Android phones only.
